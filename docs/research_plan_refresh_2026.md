# 2026 Research-Plan Refresh

**Status:** Incorporated into the canonical thesis protocols on 2026-08-27

The refresh established four durable decisions:

1. the scientific gap is source-specific cross-modal uncertainty, not generic
   vision--language uncertainty;
2. probabilistic embeddings are candidate representations rather than the
   novelty claim by themselves;
3. controlled conflict identifiability precedes calibrated risk and decision
   studies;
4. medical image--text data are the primary validation direction, subject to
   supervisor, access, and governance gates.

- [literature matrix](thesis/literature_matrix.md);

- [evaluation protocol](thesis/evaluation_protocol.md);

in parallel with those canonical documents.
